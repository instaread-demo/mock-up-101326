Iran claims US military strikes damaged 111 industrial facilities, including 60 petrochemical complexes, 44 pharmaceutical factories and seven additional pharmaceutical sites. Iranian representative Hadi Farajvand told the Organisation for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons that the attacks disrupted supply chains and threatened peaceful chemical activities. Tehran also questioned Washington’s compliance with international chemical weapons agreements. The figures remain independently unverified.
