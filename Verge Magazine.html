ARSENAL WONDERKID MAX DOWMAN MAKES HISTORY WITH THREE GUINNESS WORLD RECORDS BEFORE 17!

Arsenal wonderkid Max Dowman has officially been recognised with three Guinness World Records after his extraordinary rise through English and European football — all before his 17th birthday.

The 16-year-old Arsenal star has been presented with three official Guinness World Records certificates, marking a remarkable start to his senior career and making him the youngest player featured in the new GUINNESS WORLD RECORDS: FOOTBALL EDITION 2027.

Dowman’s record-breaking achievements have seen him make history in the Premier League and UEFA Champions League, with the teenager now taking centre stage as the first profile in the book’s new Class of ’27.

Max Dowman’s Three Guinness World Records!

Dowman has already secured three Guinness World Records titles, highlighting just how quickly the Arsenal academy graduate has made his mark on senior football.

His records are:

  • Youngest football (soccer) player to win the Premier League: 16 years, 144 days
  • Youngest goalscorer in the English Premier League: 16 years, 73 days
  • Youngest player in the UEFA Champions League: 15 years, 308 days

The three records put Dowman among a remarkable group of young players who have already made football history before reaching adulthood.

His certificates were presented to him during an exclusive presentation at Arsenal, alongside a copy of the new GUINNESS WORLD RECORDS: FOOTBALL EDITION 2027.

Dowman Made Champions League History at Just 15!

Dowman’s record-breaking journey began in Europe.

On 4 November 2025, aged just 15 years and 308 days, the Arsenal youngster came off the bench against Slavia Praha in the UEFA Champions League.

His appearance made him the youngest player ever to play in the competition, surpassing the previous record held by Borussia Dortmund’s Youssoufa Moukoko by 75 days.

Dowman also became the first 15-year-old to feature in the Champions League, underlining the extraordinary level at which his development had already reached.

For an academy player still only 15, stepping onto one of European football’s biggest stages was already a significant achievement. Breaking a long-standing age record in the process made the occasion even more memorable.

 

From Youngest Champions League Player to Premier League Record Holder!

Dowman’s historic run continued just months later when he turned his attention to the Premier League.

On 14 March 2026, the Arsenal teenager scored against Everton at the age of 16 years and 73 days, becoming the youngest goalscorer in Premier League history.

The goal added another Guinness World Records title to his rapidly growing collection and further established Dowman as one of the most exciting young talents in English football.

He would go on to claim his third record at the end of the 2025/26 season.

At 16 years and 144 days, Dowman became the youngest footballer to win the Premier League, completing an extraordinary trio of records before even turning 17.

Max Dowman Leads the Class of ’27!

Dowman’s achievements have earned him the opening player profile in the GUINNESS WORLD RECORDS: FOOTBALL EDITION 2027 Class of ’27.

The new section celebrates 27 established superstars, history-makers and breakthrough talents who are helping shape the next generation of football.

The Class of ’27 features stars from both the men’s and women’s game, bringing together established global names alongside emerging players who are already making history.

The line-up includes Lamine Yamal, Jude Bellingham, Erling Haaland, Cole Palmer, Hannah Hampton, Estêvão Willian, Manaka Matsukubo, Olivia Smith, Linda Caicedo, Trinity Rodman, Jamal Musiala, Vinícius Júnior and Kylian Mbappé, alongside Dowman.

It makes for a fascinating snapshot of football at a time when a new generation is beginning to take centre stage.

Guinness World Records Celebrates Football’s Next Generation!

According to Tom Beckerlegge, Senior Editor of Guinness World Records Football Edition 2027, Dowman perfectly represents the idea behind the new Class of ’27.

The teenager is the youngest player included in the section, with his three records before the age of 17 highlighting the pace at which the next generation is breaking through.

The new edition isn’t solely about established superstars, either. It explores the players who are already rewriting the record books alongside those whose careers could define the years ahead.

Football continues to evolve every season, with new records being set, young players breaking through and established stars finding new ways to make history. The Football Edition 2027 aims to capture that constantly changing landscape.

What’s Inside Guinness World Records: Football Edition 2027?

Alongside its Class of ’27, the new book looks at records from across the global game.

That includes achievements from the Premier League, Women’s Super League and Europe’s biggest competitions, as well as international tournaments, legendary clubs and individual players.

The book also gives younger football fans the chance to get involved themselves through interactive challenges.

One of the highlights is the Football Edition Freestyle Challenge, which features expert advice from two-time world champion Isabel Wilkins and gives readers the opportunity to test their own football skills.

So while Dowman and the other players featured in the book are already making history at the highest level, the next record-breaker could potentially be reading the book right now.

When Is Guinness World Records: Football Edition 2027 Available?

GUINNESS WORLD RECORDS: FOOTBALL EDITION 2027 is available from Thursday 8 October 2026, priced at £12.99.

The book is available in stores and online, with readers able to find a full list of retailers through the official Guinness World Records books website.

The new football edition arrives alongside the latest editions of the main Guinness World Records 2027 book and Guinness World Records: Gamer’s Edition 2027.

The main 2027 edition explores another collection of record-breaking achievements, while the Gamer’s Edition takes readers on a global journey through gaming, featuring familiar names including Mario, Link, Minecraft and Fortnite.

For football fans, however, the Football Edition 2027 offers a snapshot of a sport constantly being reshaped by its next generation — and with Max Dowman already holding three world records before turning 17, his story is only just beginning.
