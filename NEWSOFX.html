“We were really poor, and I had money. I have never been able to pay for dinner like to this day; I have never [paid for dinner.]”: Mila Kunis

What is a Dad to a kid? Dad is A son’s first hero, a daughter’s first love. Everybody talks about mother’s love, but no one talks about a father’s love and sacrifice. Taking the example of Mila and her father, which shows how a father makes every sacrifice for his children.

Mila said in a Los Angeles Times interview in 2008, on leaving Ukraine, that “It was right at the fall [of the Soviet Union.] It was very communist, and my parents wanted my brother and me to have a future, and so they just dropped everything. They came with $250.”

She also said, in her father Mark Kunis’s pride, as published on People, stating the source as HouzzTV, that “one of the most prideful men you’ll ever meet. He would never ask for a handout. And when times got tough, he just worked.”

In an old 2016 Glamour interview, she said, “My parents went through h*ll and back. They came to America with suitcases and a family of seven and $250, and that’s it. My parents, for years, worked full-time and went to college full-time. They would go to night school to learn English. My mom started working at Thrifty in Culver City as a box lady. That’s what she did until she learned English; then she became a cashier. My dad worked—f**k if I know—seven jobs? He painted a house. He would deliver toilets. He drove a cab, delivered pizzas. Whatever he could do, he did. Ultimately, my dad owned cabs, and my mom worked her way up to manager of a Rite-Aid; they bought a car and a condo. But growing up poor, I never missed out on anything. My parents did a beautiful job of not making me feel like I was lesser than any other kids.”

In a 26th November 2021 Howard Stern Interview, the Friends with Benefits actress said, “We were really poor, and I had money. I have never been able to pay for dinner. Like to this day, I have never paid for dinner. I’ll be like, ‘Let’s go out to dinner for your birthday, Dad.’ And then he calls the restaurant before we get there and puts his card down… This has been a huge problem.” She also said many things I already covered above. There is an article of mine related to this topic which you must read below.

“Men only think about 1 thing!”

Do you know the case of Norah Vincent? Nora Vincent was a radical feminist. She was an activist, and she believed that Men’s lives are easier than women’s. Men are Mean, Men are Evil, and Men are Bad. So, she decided to disguise herself as a man for two years, but just after 18 months she decided to stop the experiment because she couldn’t believe that men were being treated like tr*sh by women. She couldn’t believe that this was a thing that men had to go through every single day, and she committed s**cide.

Let me quote her words here: “Men are suffering. They have different problems than women have, but they don’t have it better. They need our sympathy, they need our love, and they need each other more than anything else. They need to be together.”

That was just an example I thought I needed to give before starting the article.

Men don’t want only one thing and think of it; rather, they worry that:

“Everyone is counting on me”/”I can’t give up”/”Just gotta keep going”/”I have to make it”

Everyone counts on a man in a family as he has to provide, protect, and take care of his wife and kids’ lives. A man’s biggest fear is being not able to provide for his family, but no man should suffer in silence. He is the last line of defense as the world is coming for his children. If he doesn’t do it, who will? He can’t fail. This is the reason why A dad is a son’s first hero and a daughter’s first love.

“I need to make more money”

Chris Rock correctly said, “Only women, children, and dogs are loved unconditionally. A man is only loved under the condition that he provides something.” A man can marry an illiterate woman earning less than him, but a woman in 99% of cases will not. They follow hypergamy. Today, liquidation of Men via Government Currency is common.

“I need to do better”

Everyone talks about a mother’s love, but no one talks about a father’s sacrifice. A dad’s heart breaks when he can’t buy his child a toy or the child’s school trip. A dad sacrifices his leisure for his family and does overtime to save money for his family.

“I’m so tired”

Is the electrician on the high-voltage pole in hard sunlight thinking about having s*x up there, or a soldier on the frontline where between the firing thinking If I survive this, I am going to have s*x later? Furthermore, be it a miner or a firefighter, everyone gets tired.
