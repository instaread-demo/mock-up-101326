Every time I open a new venue in Nivalis Nights, the first thing I want to know is which dishes the locals actually crave. Get that wrong and you’re serving plates nobody in the district cares about. This guide lays out every restaurant location alongside its three regional menu tags, so you can plan your empire expansion without guessing.

The frustrating part is that the game hides this behind a rental screen. You can’t see a district’s preferences until you’ve already committed to leasing a venue there, which is backwards for anyone trying to plan ahead. Rather than rent blind and eat the loss, I compiled everything into one list so you can decide where to build before you spend a single coin.

 

All Regional Menu Preferences

Below is the complete breakdown by location. The tags in each row are the flavour categories your menu needs to match if you want customers walking through the door happy. I grouped them in the same tier order the source uses, since the progression generally tracks how far into the map you’ve expanded.

A couple of things jump out once you line these up side by side. Ram
en Noir and Doors to Yama sit in the same district as Meridian Market and share identical preferences, so if you’ve already built a supply chain for one, the other is a cheap way to double down. Meanwhile Azure Breeze and Fly-In are the only venues running four tags instead of three, which means more flexibility — and more ingredients to juggle if you want a fully matched menu.

 

What This Actually Does For Your Profit

Matching a district’s tags isn’t just flavour text. When your menu lines up with the regional preference, you keep the locals happy and you can push your meal prices higher without tanking foot traffic. The knock-on effect is more patrons cycling through over the course of a day, which is exactly the compounding you want in a business sim.

If you’re already sourcing fish for a venue, my fishing guide pairs well with the seafood-heavy spots like Toshigami and Azure Breeze. And if noodles are your backbone — Ramen Noir, Sake Bar, and Yakitori all lean on them — worth reading the Noodle Bar guide before you commit your ingredient slots.

 

How to Check This In-Game Yourself

The data lives in the game, it’s just buried. Press TAB to open the menu, head to venue, then menu, then statistics. The catch is that you can only pull up these numbers after you’ve rented the location — until then, the preferences stay hidden. That’s the whole reason I put this list together: so you can scout the map on paper first and only sign the lease once you know the district fits your concept.

Hopefully this saves you from a few bad rentals. Good luck out there.
