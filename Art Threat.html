The album Pylon opened at No. 2 on Billboard’s Top Album Sales chart after selling 25,000 copies in the U.S. during the week ending Sept. 24, according to Luminate — the artist’s strongest sales week to date. The result arrives in a crowded chart week that underlines how physical formats and marketing tactics still shape first-week figures.

How the sales added up

Label reporting shows the release was distributed across numerous physical formats, a move that helped inflate the initial total. Collectors could choose from ten vinyl variants (some signed), three CD editions (including a signed option), two cassette editions and a digital download.

Those multiple SKUs are a familiar tactic to maximize first-week sales: limited-run and signed versions often translate into repeat purchases from dedicated fans, pushing albums higher on sales-driven charts even as streaming remains dominant overall.

Chart landscape this week

The Top Album Sales chart was especially active: nine albums debuted inside the top 10, with Miley Cyrus’ Bass Persuades taking the No. 1 spot. Other new top-10 arrivals came from a mix of country, rock and pop acts — a reminder of how diverse release schedules can crowd major chart windows.

  • Miley Cyrus — Bass Persuades (No. 1)

  • Pylon — debut at No. 2

  • Riley Green — new top-10 entry

  • Anthrax — new top-10 entry

  • Carly Rae Jepsen — new top-10 entry

  • EVAN — new top-10 entry

  • Luke Bryan — new top-10 entry

  • Lizzy McAlpin — new top-10 entry

  • Noah Kahan — new top-10 entry

While streaming charts often get more attention, the Top Album Sales list continues to reflect strategies that prioritize physical demand. For artists aiming for high chart placement, coordinating multiple product variants and signed editions remains an effective lever.

Industry watchers will be following whether these debuts sustain sales or fall back after the initial push. For now, the week’s results offer a snapshot of how release tactics and fan engagement translate into measurable chart success.
