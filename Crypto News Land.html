NEAR Posts Its Strongest Month Ever: Is $10 Finally Within Reach?

NEAR posts its strongest month ever after surging from $1.92 to $5.57. Discover whether ETF inflows can help NEAR reach the $10 target.

Near Protocol — NEAR, delivered a September that few investors expected. The token started the month at $1.92 and finished at $5.57. That move marked the strongest monthly advance in network history. Buyers pushed through several major price levels in a short period. Now comes the harder challenge. NEAR must defend recent gains while attracting fresh demand. The next few weeks may determine whether this rally continues or begins to fade.

ETF Demand Adds New Support for NEAR

After spending months between $1 and $2, NEAR finally broke higher. Buyers cleared the $2 and $3 levels before reaching above $5. More importantly, price entered a resistance area that blocked previous recoveries. Institutional interest arrived at a critical moment. The Bitwise NRR ETF attracted $35.5 million during the trading launch. The fund later added $13.2 million and about $9 million during the following sessions. Total inflows now stand near $57.7 million.

Those numbers matter because ETF creations require actual purchases of NEAR. Each new inflow creates direct spot demand. That process can help support price during pullbacks. Market activity remained healthy during the same period. NEAR gained 1.7% while spot trading volume reached $444.7 million. Network data also showed $457.6K in repurchases over seven days.

Strong ETF demand could help buyers maintain control. Continued inflows would also strengthen confidence in September’s breakout. The current pullback therefore deserves close attention. NEAR recently traded around $4.73 after reaching higher levels. Such corrections often occur after strong rallies. Investors now watch whether buyers step in and defend support.

Can NEAR Hold the Breakout and Target $10?

Technical structure remains constructive despite recent weakness. NEAR spent a long period trading between $0.861 and $1.80. September’s surge changed that picture dramatically. Resistance now sits near the $6 to $6.40 region. Previous rallies struggled in that area. Buyers must overcome that barrier before another major advance begins. A decline toward $4.78 does not automatically signal trouble.

Instead, current price action tests market conviction. Profit-taking often follows strong monthly gains. Support between $4.50 and $4.69 remains a key zone. Holding above that range would keep the bullish outlook intact. Such a move could open the door toward $8.30 and eventually $10. The opposite scenario also deserves consideration. A break below $4.50 could weaken bullish momentum.

Sellers would likely gain confidence if support fails. For now, NEAR stands at an important crossroads. September proved buyers can drive powerful gains. ETF inflows continue adding demand. If support holds and resistance breaks, the path toward $10 could become a realistic target rather than a distant possibility.
