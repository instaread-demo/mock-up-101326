Cllr Scanlon, independent, said: “We need an urgent meeting of all stakeholders to discuss the junction at Glenmore… A local business is willing to carry out the work and possibly purchase the land. It’s not every day we get an offer like that.”

The junction sits between Ballybofey and Glenfin.
