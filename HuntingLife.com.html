Christensen Arms Names Kalvyn Ferronato Senior Marketing Manager

Kevin Paulson6 seconds ago
0 266 1 minute read

Christensen Arms, the leader in precision firearms, today announced the appointment of Kalvyn Ferronato as Senior Marketing Manager.

Ferronato brings nearly a decade of experience in marketing, public relations and strategic communications, with a career spanning government, conservation, and the outdoor and firearms industries. Over the course of her career, she has taken on progressively greater responsibilities across brand strategy, media relations, integrated marketing campaigns, product launches and client leadership. Her experience includes developing strategic communications programs, managing key media and industry relationships, guiding brand messaging and executing campaigns designed to strengthen brand awareness and connect with consumers. 

Ferronato also brings an established understanding of Christensen Arms, having previously worked with the company while managing its account at Gunpowder Inc. In that role, she developed relationships with the team and an in-depth understanding of the brand and its products.

“Kalvyn knows this brand and the people behind it,” said Daniel Vernon, Head of Marketing at Christensen Arms. “She brings a fresh perspective to a story she already understands, and I’m excited to work with her to bring that story to life.”

In her role, Ferronato will develop and execute marketing campaigns, guide brand communications, and create content across Christensen Arms’ product portfolio.

“Having worked with Christensen Arms previously, I’ve had the opportunity to get to know the team, the brand and the products firsthand,” said Ferronato. “I’m excited to build on those relationships, contribute in a new way and help tell the Christensen Arms story.”

For more information on Christensen Arms, visit www.christensenarms.com.

About Christensen Arms: Christensen Arms, based in Gunnison, Utah, is a premier manufacturer of precision firearms and custom carbon-fiber barrels. Rooted in innovation and craftsmanship, the company equips hunters, shooters, and tactical enthusiasts with world-class tools—all proudly Made in the USA. Discover more at www.christensenarms.com.
