Ryan Reynolds FUMES Over Blake Lively Scandal in Unsealed Texts

In late 2024, Blake Lively became the target of online ridicule — months before her legal battle began and the world learned what was really going on.

Recently, rumors have circulated that she and husband Ryan Reynolds are in troubled waters.

Can a look at the actor’s unsealed texts shed light on where things stand?

‘Obviously a predator and sociopath’

Newly unsealed court documents show a text exchange between Reynolds and author Colleen Hoover.

Hoover is a sometimes polarizing author, but she is relevant here for having written It Ends With Us — the book that spawned the film adaptation that turned Lively’s life upside down.

Page Six got ahold of texts from August 10, 2024 in which Reynolds is clearly alluding to Justin Baldoni.

“It’s amazing to me that you can have everybody involved with a production completely run away from a person who is obviously a predator and sociopath,” he lamented.

“And,” Reynolds continued, “the narrative must first land on why a woman is the problem.”

Before any of the drama first erupted on TikTok, people had already noticed that none of the It Ends With Us cast seemed eager to speak about Baldoni on the red carpet.

Remember, he was not only one of the stars, but also the film’s director. His absence from promotions, and the clear disinterest in discussing him, was very conspicuous.

“It’s amazing to me how low people still go,” Reynolds remarked in the texts.

“But people are already coming forward,” he continued “and soon enough someone is just gonna go on record.”

It seems that at the time, he and Lively had not yet reached the decision to file a claim against Baldoni.

‘The really smart folks already know’

Colleen Hoover responded in agreement — but she also shared her perspective with Reynolds about Lively’s online critics.

“It’s mind-blowing,” Hoover echoed.

“But,” she continued, “also I feel like the loud ones are mostly 13-year-olds who don’t have fully developed brains yet and will be mortified by their thought process a few years from now.”

As with other, similar cases, a tremendous number of people online have come forward to admit that they regretted being hoodwinked by online caricatures of Lively.

“Good to hear it’s already beginning to skew away from him,” Hoover added, another apparent reference to Baldoni.

“Surprising it’s mostly girls,” Reynolds remarked. Many expect misogyny to spread most by and among men, but patriarchy is more insidious than that. “At least, that’s what I’ve seen.”

He continued: “And the really smart folks already know. Although the narrative at the moment is how insanely well the film is performing and how much audiences love it. So…”

A source close to Reynolds spoke to Page Six, saying: “It is no secret that Ryan was trying to protect his wife, who was being targeted by a crisis PR team hired by Baldoni.”

The source added: “This is the same crisis team who has been accused of these smear tactics in at least 5 other lawsuits according to the NYT.”

Though this was from over two years ago, these texts are from the height of the hate campaign against Lively. If Reynolds was standing by his wife this adamantly before the public learned the truth about what she experienced on Baldoni’s set, one would imagine that the injustices that she faced in and out of court did not sour things.

RJ Mitte Cause of Death: 'Breaking Bad' Star Was 34

By Author Tyler Johnson

Published: Oct 12, 2026 at 4:46 PM • Category Breaking News

Reading Time: 2 minutes

Reading Time: 2 minutes

We have tragic news to report from the world of television today.

RJ Mitte — the actor best known for his role as Walter White Jr. on Breaking Bad — has passed away.

He was just 34 years old.

The news comes courtesy of a report from TMZ.

A county official confirmed to the outlet that the actor died Monday in Texas. One insider tells TMZ that Mitte died by suicide.

The news comes after Mitte recently reflected on the television series that helped make him a familiar face to millions of viewers.

Just last week, he appeared on “The Joe Vulpis Podcast,” where he discussed how Breaking Bad changed his life and spoke about maintaining close relationships with his castmates years after the show ended.

For fans who watched Walter White’s transformation from mild-mannered chemistry teacher to drug kingpin, Mitte was an integral part of the story.

His character, Walter White Jr., provided an important connection to the family life that Walter’s increasingly dangerous choices threatened to destroy.

Mitte appeared in 56 episodes of the acclaimed AMC drama, which remains one of television’s most celebrated series.

Earlier this month, Mitte also spent time with his Breaking Bad co-star Luis Moncada. The pair appeared to be in good spirits in social media posts, sharing smiles during their reunion.

Outside of his signature role, Mitte built a résumé that included appearances in The Oak Room, The Recall, and The Guardians of Justice (Will Save You!).

The actor also had major personal milestones ahead of him. Mitte was engaged to Kennedy Suarez, his longtime girlfriend, after proposing last year. The couple had been dating for years before taking that next step in their relationship.

Mitte’s death marks a devastating loss for fans of Breaking Bad, a series that continues to attract new viewers long after its finale. His performance as Walter White Jr. helped bring emotional weight to a story about a father whose double life increasingly endangered the people closest to him.

While Mitte’s work extended beyond the role that introduced him to audiences, his place in the Breaking Bad universe remained a defining part of his career.

Our thoughts are with Mitte’s loved ones, friends, and colleagues during this enormously difficult time.
